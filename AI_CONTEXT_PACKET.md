# Context Briefing: BuddyBoardz 10 (GSEB Class 10 Board Companion)

### 1. Who We Are & The Vision
We are building **BuddyBoardz 10** (`buddyboardz10.pages.dev`), an offline-first, zero-login, high-taste web companion and PWA for GSEB Class 10 (SSC) English-medium students in Gujarat (Surat, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, Rajkot).

### 2. The Positioning Wedge
"Turn any GSEB Class 10 paper test into a 3-minute marks-recovery plan."

### 3. The Core Philosophy: "Book + Pen + Brain" (Anti-Screen Trap)
The GSEB Board exam is a **3-hour handwritten test written with a physical pen on paper**.
Most EdTech apps fail students by trapping them in 45-minute video feeds, gamified candy-crush streaks, and screen addiction.
**BuddyBoardz believes the opposite:** Technology must be a 3-minute catalytic utility that steps completely out of the way so students spend 95% of their revision time with physical textbooks, notebooks, and pens.

### 4. The Two Distinct Surfaces
1. **The Web Hub (`buddyboardz.pages.dev` / landing page):**
   - Public brand manifesto & "Book + Pen + Brain" philosophy.
   - PWA installation guide (iOS "Add to Home Screen" & Android PWA setup).
   - Open-source GitHub repository link.
   - Blueprint breakdown & study guides.
2. **The App (`buddyboardz10.pages.dev` / PWA):**
   - 4-tab lightweight offline mobile utility:
     - **Tab 1: 109-Mark Simulator:** Visualizes GSEB's 29-mark general option buffer across 64 verified chapters (Maths Standard 12, Maths Basic 18, Science 11, Social Science 10).
     - **Tab 2: 3-Minute 4-Tap Logger:** Log any paper autopsy in <180s (`Chapter` → `Section A/B/C/D` → `Marks Lost 1/2/3/4` → `Error Tag`).
     - **Tab 3: Priority Radar & Landmine Checklist:** High-ROI chapters to revise + top execution traps (`SILLY`/`TIME`).
     - **Tab 4: Spaced Retest Queue:** Client-side FSRS-6 scheduler for `CONCEPT` and `FORMULA` mistakes, strictly capped to `Exam Date - 2 days`.

### 5. The Main Problem & 5 Root Failure Modes
- **The Main Problem:** The "Mistake Discard Loop" — students solve 20+ tuition/mock tests (Gala, Navneet, Allen, school prelims), see a score (e.g. 54/80), feel bad, stuff the paper in their bag, and repeat the same 15 marks of mistakes on the final board exam.
- **Problem 1 (Autopsy Friction):** Analyzing mistakes by hand takes 45 minutes; students abandon it.
- **Problem 2 (Option Blindness):** Students study all chapters equally, unaware that GSEB prints 109 marks to score 80 (29M buffer) and has 24 compulsory Section A marks.
- **Problem 3 (Review Collapse):** Passive textbook re-reading creates an illusion of competence; mistakes are forgotten within 72 hours without spaced retrieval.
- **Problem 4 (Conflating Slips with Gaps):** Calculation slips and sign errors are treated like flashcards; students need a Pre-Exam Landmine Checklist, not 50 flashcards.
- **Problem 5 (Screen Trap):** Students spend hours on phones instead of practicing handwritten answers for the 3-hour physical board exam.
