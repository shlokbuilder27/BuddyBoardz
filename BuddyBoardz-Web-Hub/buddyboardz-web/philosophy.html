THE BELIEF BEHIND THE PRODUCT

Technology should
know when to step back.

BuddyBoardz is built around a simple idea: the screen should help you find the work, not become the work.

OUR PHILOSOPHYBOOK + PEN + BRAIN
01 — THE CONTRADICTION

Exams happen on paper.
So should most of the preparation.

The GSEB board examination is a three-hour handwritten test. Students need to recall, structure, calculate, draw, and write under time pressure. A study tool should respect that reality.

We do not believe another endless stream of videos, streaks, or notifications is the answer. We believe a small amount of well-designed technology can make offline study more deliberate.

THE PRINCIPLE

Use the screen to find
the next page. Then close it.

02 — THE THREE PARTS
01

Book — understand the why.

Return to the source. Read the explanation. Connect the missing idea rather than memorising a correction without context.

02

Pen — practise the doing.

Write the solution. Show the steps. Practise the exact kind of work the examination will ask you to perform.

03

Brain — retrieve and retain.

Close the book and recall. Revisit conceptual mistakes after a little time has passed, so learning is tested rather than merely recognised.

03 — OUR NON-NEGOTIABLES

Small by design.
Clear by default.

01 / No attention traps

No infinite feed, streak pressure, or engagement tricks.

02 / No unnecessary account

The v1 product is designed to work without a login.

03 / Transparent data

Explain local storage, backups, and limitations plainly.

04 / Useful in minutes

Help students decide what to do, then let them do it.

Book + Pen + Brain.

Technology that supports the desk — and respects the student sitting at it.
