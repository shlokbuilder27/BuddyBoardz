# PROBLEM.md — The Core Problem & 5 Root Failure Modes in GSEB Class 10

**Project:** `BuddyBoardz 10`  
**Target:** GSEB Class 10 (SSC) English-Medium Students (Surat & Gujarat)  
**Exam Context:** 80-Mark Written Theory Exam (3 Hours, Pen on Paper) + 20-Mark Internal = 100 Marks. Passing Floor: 26/80 in Theory.

---

## The Main Problem: The "Mistake Discard" Loop

Every Class 10 student in Gujarat solves dozens of mock papers between November and February:
- School prelims and unit tests
- Weekly Sunday tuition tests (Allen, Aakash, local tuition batches)
- Gala Model Question Papers (Navneet) & Kumar paper sets

**The Universal Failure Loop:**
1. The student gets the evaluated paper back with red ink: e.g., `54 / 80`.
2. They feel a burst of anxiety or frustration for 10 minutes.
3. They look at the total marks, maybe compare with a classmate.
4. **The paper gets stuffed into the bottom of their school bag, never to be opened again.**
5. The student goes back to passively reading textbooks or watching YouTube lectures.
6. **Result:** On the final GSEB Board Exam in March, they make the **exact same 12 to 18 marks of mistakes** that they already made in December and January.

> **Core Insight:** Board marks are not lost because students don't study; marks are lost because **unresolved mock mistakes compound invisibly**.

---

## The 5 Root Problems (Why Current Solutions Fail)

### Problem 1: The "Autopsy Friction" Barrier
* **The Reality:** Analyzing a 54-question board paper by hand requires categorizing every error, finding the chapter, and calculating marks lost. It takes 30–45 minutes of tedious administrative work.
* **Why it Fails:** After sitting for a 3-hour test, no 15-year-old has the mental bandwidth to write a 45-minute journal.
* **BuddyBoardz Solution:** A **3-Minute, 4-Tap Logger** (`Chapter` → `Section A/B/C/D` → `Marks Lost (1/2/3/4)` → `Error Tag`). Under 180 seconds to log an entire test.

### Problem 2: Blueprint & General-Option Blindness (The "Equal Effort" Trap)
* **The Reality:** The GSEB Class 10 paper prints **109 marks** for a student to score **80 marks** (a massive **29-mark general option buffer** across Sections B, C, and D). Meanwhile, Section A has **24 compulsory 1-mark questions with zero options**.
* **Why it Fails:** Students treat all chapters equally. They spend 20 hours grinding difficult 3-mark chapters while neglecting high-yield 14-mark giants (e.g., Statistics, Life Processes, Linear Equations) or failing Section A passing criteria.
* **BuddyBoardz Solution:** **109-Mark General-Option Simulator**. Live calculation of achievable board score based on which chapters are Mastered, Section-A-Only, or Skipped.

### Problem 3: The "Review Collapse" & Illusion of Competence
* **The Reality:** When a student loses marks on a concept (e.g., Quadratic formula discriminant or Fleming's Left-Hand Rule), they re-read that page in the textbook. Because the page looks familiar, the brain creates an **illusion of competence**.
* **Why it Fails:** Without active spaced retrieval, the human forgetting curve wipes out the correction within 48 to 72 hours.
* **BuddyBoardz Solution:** Client-side **FSRS-6 Spaced Retest Queue**. Only for `CONCEPT` and `FORMULA` errors. Automatically caps all review intervals to `Exam Date - 2 days` so no card is scheduled after the board exam.

### Problem 4: Conflating "Silly Slips" with "Conceptual Ignorance"
* **The Reality:** Over 40% of marks lost in Maths and Science are **execution traps**:
  - Sign flips (`-` becoming `+` when opening brackets)
  - Copying wrong values from the question paper
  - Forgetting units (`cm²`, `m/s`, `Ω`)
  - Running out of time in Section D
* **Why it Fails:** Existing flashcard apps treat a sign slip like a flashcard. Doing flashcards for a calculation slip is useless and causes burnout.
* **BuddyBoardz Solution:** **Two-Bucket Engine**:
  - `CONCEPT` / `FORMULA` → Routed to Spaced Retest Queue.
  - `SILLY` / `TIME` → Routed to the **Pre-Exam Landmine Checklist** (a top-5 personalized warning sheet reviewed 30 minutes before entering the exam hall).

### Problem 5: The Screen Addiction Trap vs. Paper Board Reality
* **The Reality:** The GSEB Board Exam is a **physical 3-hour examination written with a ballpoint pen on paper**.
* **Why it Fails:** Modern EdTech apps (Byju's, PhysicsWallah, Quizlet) are designed to maximize screen time, video views, and dopamine loops. Students end up scrolling on a glowing phone instead of practicing with pen and paper.
* **BuddyBoardz Solution:** **Book + Pen + Brain Manifesto**. BuddyBoardz is a 3-minute catalytic utility. It gives the student their diagnostic mission, then **completely steps out of the way** so the student does 95% of their studying on physical paper with physical books.
